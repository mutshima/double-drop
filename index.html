Need help?

Write to us at doubledrop.game@gmail.com. Tell us your iPhone model and the app version (at the bottom of Settings in the game). We answer within a few days.

Common questions

I bought coins but didn't get them.
Close the game fully and open it again while you're online: a purchase that didn't arrive is checked again when the game starts. If the coins are still missing, write to us with the date and the order ID from Apple's receipt email, and we'll add them.
How do I get the starter pack back on a new phone?
Open the shop and tap Restore purchases. Coin packs you already used can't be restored, because the coins were spent.
How do I get a refund?
Apple handles every payment, so refunds go through Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?
Yes. Progress is kept only on your phone, so deleting the app deletes it.
