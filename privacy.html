Privacy policy

Double Drop is a puzzle game by Lotus Software Solutions SRL ("we"). This page says what the game collects, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version

  • You don't make an account. We never ask for your name, email or contacts.
  • We collect how the game is played (levels, hints, purchases) to make the levels better.
  • We collect crash reports to fix bugs.
  • You can turn both off in the game: Settings → Share anonymous usage data.
  • We don't show ads and don't sell or share your data for advertising. We don't track you across other apps.

What we collect

Gameplay data. Which levels you start, win or leave, stars, hints and undos used, when you open the shop and what you buy. Each phone gets a random ID made by the game, not linked to your name, Apple ID or Google account. Along with it: device model, iOS or Android version, app version, and language. We don't keep your IP address. We use the PostHog analytics service, with data stored in the European Union.

Crash reports. When the game crashes or hits an error, a report with the error, device model, iOS or Android version and app version is sent to us through PostHog and Sentry.

Purchases. On iPhone, Apple processes every payment; on Android, Google Play does (Google Play Billing). We never see your card, Apple ID or Google account. To confirm a purchase and give you your coins, the game sends the store's receipt (Apple's, or Google Play's purchase token) to RevenueCat, which keeps the purchase history for that random ID.

Game Center (iPhone only). If you're signed in to Apple's Game Center, the game sends your achievements and leaderboard scores to Apple, and Apple handles that data under its own privacy policy. The game never sends your Game Center name or ID to us or anyone else.

Reminders. Reminders are scheduled and stored on your phone. We don't use a push notification service or collect push tokens. If Share anonymous usage data is on, we can see whether reminders are enabled and whether you tapped one, but not what it said.

On your phone only. Your progress, coins, stars and settings stay on your phone and are not sent to us.

Turning it off

Open Settings in the game and turn off Share anonymous usage data. The game then stops sending gameplay data and crash reports. Purchases still go through RevenueCat, because that's how you get your coins.

Why we use it

To run the game and deliver purchases, to find and fix bugs, and to see which levels are too hard or too easy. Our legal basis under the GDPR is our legitimate interest in running and improving the game, and the purchase contract for purchases.

Who processes it for us

  • PostHog Inc. – analytics and error reports (EU servers)
  • Functional Software Inc. (Sentry) – crash reports (EU servers)
  • RevenueCat Inc. – purchase checking
  • Apple – payments, the App Store and Game Center (iPhone)
  • Google – payments and Google Play (Android)

They use the data only to provide their service to us. Some of them may process data in the United States under the EU standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

Gameplay and crash data for up to 13 months, then it is deleted. Purchase records as long as the law requires for accounting.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by writing to doubledrop.game@gmail.com. Since we don't know who you are, include the app version and the date you played so we can find the right data. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in Romania it is ANSPDCP.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has sent us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update it here and change the date at the top.
